# Continuous office blocks

The screenshot showed freestanding source-sized buildings surrounded by large paving slabs and grass gaps. The office layout now partitions the land between streets, rather than placing randomly sized rectangles along each road with a four-metre gap.

`web/features/office-blocks.js` subtracts the road/sidewalk boundary from unlocked land. Office development removes the old extra 1.5 m development setback, while retaining the actual road boundary (8.3 m from a default street centre). Residential setbacks and visible road geometry are unchanged. Enclosed components become blocks. Open roads receive a continuous 64 m deep development belt. Balanced cuts parallel to the longest boundary divide the polygon into lots, usually about 30–66 m across, distributing remaining space instead of skipping it. Narrow remainders merge with touching neighbours. Concave, angled and curved boundaries use polygon intersections, not overlapping bounding rectangles.

Bucket fill selects all lots with the clicked site's block identity. The brush still paints individual lots. Different blocks can have different sizes and lot counts. Water, excessive terrain slope, locked land and landmarks remain exclusions; this is not permission to build through them. Layout caches retain the existing road, terrain, bounds and landmark invalidation rules. Landmark transactions keep occupied sites stable.

Every rebuilt design has an actual textured ground-floor volume occupying the entire lot, up to two storeys tall (or the full building body for shorter designs). This replaces the exposed concrete slab around the building. The upper architecture keeps its reference height and is uniformly fitted within its site; it is neither stretched separately along X/Z nor allowed to cross a neighbouring lot. Mixed selection ranks reference plan areas against the lot area, then deterministically chooses among 16 suitable designs. Explicit design choices remain available on every office lot.

The authored survey bands previously included offsets, expanding profiles and separate sections that did not reliably support upper parts. Cached support plans now union each upper volume into the bands beneath it. Compound overlapping plans are unioned before extrusion, courtyard walls face into their courtyards, and courtyard holes are excluded from terrace/roof caps. Setbacks, wings, curves, crowns, facade materials and reference heights remain. This makes some widening or leaning silhouettes more vertical; it deliberately trades unsupported survey artifacts for continuous structure. Roof frames now join their lintels, angled-roof fans sit on equipment housings, crown sizes fit their supporting roofs, and structural roof pieces remain present at all detail levels.

The unchanged v52 hash remains mandatory. Tests cover complete coverage and non-overlap for rectangular, rotated and concave blocks, lot containment, the sidewalk boundary, supporting plans for all 98 designs, full-lot textured street walls, bucket/brush differences, catalogue repainting, demolition and undo/redo. Existing residential, rendering and landmark checks remain. Some older tests deliberately changed from native isolated parcels/single-site bucket fill to the newly requested whole-block behavior.

For visual development checks, `node scripts/create-office-block-preview.cjs` generates an ignored fixture using the real road network, zoning and neighborhood building renderer. Its street slabs are inspection aids, not replacements for the game's road system. It and the local reference comparison page are excluded from builds/exports. Full-city FPS and a playthrough of every road/terrain configuration are not established by these checks.

To test the new version, open the newly exported **Pancake City - Filled Office Blocks Playtest.html** in Downloads. Select **Zoning → Office → Bucket fill** and click within a block formed by surface streets. Use **Mix all 98** for varied development. Refill older Office zones because the former scattered plot boundaries differ from the new layout. Earlier playtests and the immutable original remain untouched.
